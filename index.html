Resonance

Read minds. Hit the bullseye.

Cooperative mode

Everyone plays as one team. No left/right. Team names are hidden.

How to Play

  1. Psychic secretly sees a target on the spectrum and gives one clue that fits that position between the two extremes.
  2. Team discusses and moves the dial to where they think the target is.
  3. Other team (optional) guesses whether the true target is left or right of the dial.
  4. Reveal — closer to the center = more points (4 / 3 / 2).

First to 10 points wins. If a team scores 4 and is still behind, they take another turn (catch-up rule).

Clue rules: one thought, real-world, on-topic. No inventing things or using the spectrum words.
